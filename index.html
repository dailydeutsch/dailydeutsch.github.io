      Install Täglich Wort

      One tap, and the word of the day sits on your home screen.

      1. Open this page in Safari then tap the Share button .
      2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. If you do not see it, scroll to the bottom, tap Edit Actions, then tap + next to Add to Home Screen.
      3. Tap Add. Leave Open as Web App on, so it opens as an app. Täglich Wort will appear on your home screen.
      4. Works in Chrome, Firefox and Edge too: Share, then Add to Home Screen. Apple's guide
      1. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top corner of Chrome.
      2. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
      3. Tap Install to confirm.
