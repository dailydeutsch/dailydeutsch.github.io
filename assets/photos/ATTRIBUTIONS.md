# Photo attributions

All photos in this folder are real photos from Wikimedia Commons, public domain or
Creative Commons licensed (CC BY, CC BY-SA or CC0). No AI-generated images are used.
The photo of the day is picked by day of year, so the same photo shows all day.

License notes: CC BY and CC BY-SA photos require attribution, given below and in the
app caption. CC0 and public domain photos need no attribution but are credited anyway.
